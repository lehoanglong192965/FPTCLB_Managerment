=== TABLE 0 (3x2) ===
R000 | EV Driver || An EV Driver is the primary user of the charging-station ecosystem who locates charging stations on the in-web map, books and manages charging sessions, starts and terminates sessions via Web/QR, tracks charge progress and receives notifications, and completes payments via Vnpay platform. There are several hundred to thousands of potential EV Owners as the system scales; regular users may charge multiple times per week with usage spikes at commuting hours and before long trips. EV Drivers typically require no formal training (in-app guidance only), need reliable low-latency map and session flows (e.g., booking success ≥98%, map load <2s).
R001 | Station Staff || Station Staff are on-site operators responsible for daily station operations including monitoring station health and alarms, assisting users with session start/stop or manual overrides, performing basic hardware resets and maintenance tasks, and updating local logs through a staff dashboard. Staff interact with the system daily and use a staff web dashboard most will require onboarding and periodic training for hardware/software procedures. Their role needs offline-capable workflows and fast alarm notification latency (real-time or near real-time).
R002 | Administrator || System Administrators configure backend systems, manage user permissions and security policies, monitor system health dashboards and run analytics and predictive planning (demand forecasts, station utilization). They typically operate during business hours with on-call responsibilities for outages, require elevated access to system consoles and logs, and depend on reliable telemetry and data integrity for model training and validation. They also require access controls, audit trails for critical operations, and tools for anomaly detection and incident response.
